This policy explains what information the game Hangzhou Tycoon ("the Game", "we") handles when you play it in a web browser, and what our advertising partner does.

1. Information we store

The Game runs entirely in your browser. We do not operate a user account system, and we do not upload your game progress to a server.

  • Local game progress is saved in your browser's localStorage under the key hzmonopoly_v02. It contains only game state (month, cash, owned plots). It never leaves your device and is deleted when you clear your browser data.
  • No personal data is collected by us: no name, email, phone number, precise location, contacts, or photos.

2. Advertising

The Game is free and supported by advertising delivered by Google (Google AdSense / Google H5 Games Ads). Google and its partners may use cookies and device identifiers to serve and measure ads.

  • Google's practices: How Google uses information from sites that use its services
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings.
  • Where required by law (EEA, UK, Switzerland), we ask for your consent through a Google-certified consent management platform before personalised ads are shown. You can change or withdraw consent at any time from the consent link in the game footer.
  • If you decline personalised advertising, you will still see non-personalised ads and the Game remains fully playable.

3. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information to an advertising partner, please contact us and we will assist in removal.

4. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data, most requests can be handled locally: clearing your browser storage removes all game data. For data held by our advertising partner, use the Google links above or contact us and we will forward your request.

5. Changes

If this policy changes materially, we will update the effective date above and, where appropriate, show a notice in the Game.
